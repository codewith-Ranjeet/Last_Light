# 📜 Last Light — Story Bible (v1.0)

## The Fall

There was a time when the world was filled with light.

Across every coast stood great lighthouses whose flames burned day and night. They were more than navigational beacons—they were ancient wards, pushing back a mysterious force known only as **The Darkness**.

No one knew where the Darkness came from.

No one knew what it wanted.

People only knew one truth:

> **Where the light ended, the Darkness began.**

For centuries, the balance held.

Then, one by one...

The lights began to die.

Some lighthouses collapsed.

Others were abandoned.

Many simply... went silent.

As each beacon faded, the Darkness consumed another piece of the world.

Cities vanished.

Villages disappeared.

Roads, forests, mountains—even memories of those places—were swallowed whole.

Eventually, only one light remained.

---

## The Last Lighthouse

Far from the fallen kingdoms, upon a lonely island surrounded by an endless black sea, stands the final lighthouse.

Its flame has burned longer than anyone can remember.

It is the final barrier between humanity and oblivion.

If this light goes out...

There will be nothing left.

---

## The Keeper

You are not a hero.

You are not a king.

You are not a chosen one.

You are simply...

**The Keeper.**

The last soul entrusted with protecting the final lighthouse.

Every sunrise is another day borrowed.

Every sunset is another battle.

You do not fight to win a war.

You fight to survive one more night.

---

## The Darkness

The Darkness is not weather.

It is not fog.

It is not smoke.

It is something far older.

Something alive.

During the day, sunlight weakens its grip on the world, forcing it to retreat beyond the horizon.

As dusk falls, it slowly creeps back across the land.

By night, it surrounds the island completely.

From within it emerge creatures made of shadow—silent, relentless, drawn only to the lighthouse.

They do not hunt the Keeper.

They seek the flame.

---

## The Shadow Creatures

The creatures have no names.

No voices.

No fear.

They are fragments of the Darkness itself.

When dawn arrives, they cannot remain in the light.

As the first rays of the sun reach the island, they abandon their assault and retreat into the Darkness once more, waiting for another night.

---

## The Beacons

Every shadow destroyed leaves behind a small fragment of light.

No one knows why.

Some believe these are pieces of souls the Darkness consumed.

Others believe they are remnants of ancient lighthouse flames.

The Keeper simply calls them **Beacons**.

These fragments fuel repairs, strengthen the lighthouse, and keep the flame burning for another night.

---

## The Four Phases

### ☀ Day

The Darkness has retreated.

The Keeper repairs the lighthouse, gathers Beacons, and prepares for the coming night.

---

### 🌇 Dusk

The sun begins to fade.

The air grows cold.

The ocean falls silent.

The Keeper has only moments left to prepare.

---

### 🌑 Night

The Darkness surrounds the island.

Shadow creatures emerge from beyond the light.

Protect the lighthouse.

Survive.

---

### 🌅 Dawn

The first light returns.

The Darkness recoils.

Any surviving shadows retreat into the void from which they came.

The Keeper has survived another night.

For now.

---

# The Core Theme

This isn't a story about defeating evil.

It's about **endurance**.

The Keeper doesn't expect victory.

There may never be one.

His purpose is simple:

> **Keep the flame alive for one more night.**

Because as long as the Last Light burns...

The world still has hope.
